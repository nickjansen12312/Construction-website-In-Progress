OUR COMMITMENT

Safety First

SAFETY IS HOW WE WORK

Everyone deserves to go home safely.

Safety isn't a separate part of our work. It is built into every decision we make, from planning and prefabrication to installation and final completion.

Our teams are responsible for looking out for themselves, their coworkers, our partners, and everyone who interacts with our jobsites.

01

Plan Ahead

We identify risks before work begins and make sure our teams have the information and resources they need.

02

Train Continuously

Ongoing training helps our people recognize hazards and make informed decisions in the field.

03

Speak Up

Every team member has a responsibility to raise concerns and stop when something doesn't look right.

04

Take Responsibility

Safety belongs to everyone. We hold ourselves and one another accountable for maintaining a safe workplace.
